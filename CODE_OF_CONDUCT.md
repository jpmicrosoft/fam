# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, professional, and
constructive. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- focus feedback on the work rather than the person;
- welcome differing experience levels and technical perspectives;
- communicate clearly and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact;
- protect private, customer, security, and personal information; and
- accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, release channels, and other spaces used to represent this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, `jpmicrosoft`,
through a trusted channel available to you. Do not place personal, security, or
other sensitive details in a public issue.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community and project. Reports will be reviewed as privately and consistently
as the available information permits.
